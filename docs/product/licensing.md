# Licensing

FatLeon commerce software is sold as one codebase under two commercial licenses. The receipt names the SKU, Standard or Agency, and the purchase archive contains the matching license file, version 2.2. That file and the receipt govern the order. This page explains how to choose. The published summary, and the license text itself, are on the [licensing reference](https://docs.fatleon.com/reference/licensing).

If this page and the license file disagree, the license file controls use of the software. Terms of sale control purchase, payment, and refunds. The receipt controls which SKU you bought. Price sits on the product page, not in the license file.

## Which license fits

Buy Standard when the stores belong to you or your company. Buy Agency when you deliver stores to other businesses. Client work is not covered by Standard.

## What each one covers

| | Standard | Agency |
|---|---|---|
| Who it is for | A brand, a startup, or an in-house team | A freelancer, a studio, or an integrator |
| Storefronts | As many as you or your company own and operate | As many client storefronts as you build and deliver |
| How you pay | Once. The right to use it does not expire | Once. The right to use it does not expire |
| Work for clients | Not this license | Included |
| Give the client the project repository | No | Yes, for that one storefront. You grant an End Client Project License. It is a sublicense from you, not a new license from the seller |
| Who may keep maintaining the client's store | — | Authorized Maintainers, and only that store |
| Where it is hosted | Your infrastructure | Yours or the client's. You remain responsible for operations and compliance |
| Move the license to another company | Only with written consent | Only with written consent |
| Sell the Fatleon Commerce source | Not allowed | Not allowed |

## Everyday use

Under Standard you can run the stores you operate, change the source, keep it in a private Git repository, and use AI tools. Your team can work in that repository. Each store can have a staging or preview environment. Moving an existing store to another domain does not require another purchase.

Under Agency you can deliver as many customized client stores as you need, including white-label work, and deploy them on your AWS account or the client's. You may hand over the customized source for one storefront under the End Client Project License. The client can name Authorized Maintainers — employees, operators, or another agency — for that store only. The client does not own the kit source and cannot start other stores from it. Who owns the custom work is settled in your contract with the client, not in this license.

## Limits that apply to both

Do not resell, sublicense, or redistribute the kit source as a template, boilerplate, starter, or competing product. Do not place the kit source in a public repository or on a file share. Do not offer a SaaS product or a site builder that gives other people substantially the same Fatleon Commerce functionality, unless the seller has licensed that offering separately. Do not assign the license to another person or company without written consent.

## If the license ends

The license lasts unless it ends for a material breach, such as publishing the kit source. After that, new projects and new deployments that use the kit source stop. Stores that are already live may keep running, and an end client's project license continues for a store that was already delivered. Backup, migration, and maintenance of those stores can continue. That copy of the source is not a starting point for an unrelated store or a competing product.
